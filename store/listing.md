## Long description — Play Store (4000 char max)

Roughly 1,750 characters, well inside the limit.

```
Pirith Pothaa brings the Pirith chanting tradition to your phone, in a form
made for everyday listening.

Open the app and play. Let the chanting continue while you cook, travel,
work or fall asleep — the audio keeps going when you leave the app or lock
your screen, with full controls on your lock screen, in your notification
shade, and through Bluetooth headphones or your car stereo.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Listen to Pirith chants, from short suttas to the full Maha Piritha
• Keep listening with the screen off or the app closed
• Control playback from the lock screen, notifications, or Bluetooth
• Download chants and play them with no connection at all
• Build your own playlists and play them straight through or shuffled
• Repeat a single chant, or loop a whole playlist
• Set a sleep timer so the chanting stops on its own
• Save favourites and return to what you played recently
• Browse by category, or search by name

SINHALA AND ENGLISH TOGETHER

Every screen shows both languages. Chant names appear in Sinhala with the
English name beneath, so the app suits Sinhala speakers and anyone learning.
Choose which language leads from Settings, and the app remembers.

MADE FOR LISTENING, NOT FOR SCROLLING

One tap plays a chant. The player is calm and uncluttered: large artwork, a
clear progress bar, and controls where your thumb already is. Nothing gets in
the way of the chanting.

WORKS OFFLINE

Downloaded chants live on your device. Once a chant is saved you can play it
on a flight, in a signal-free area, or without using mobile data again.

PRIVACY

No account. No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
Your playlists, favourites, history and downloads stay on your device and are
never uploaded.

The app is supported by ads.

May the blessings of the Pirith be with you.
```

## Long description — App Store (4000 char max)

App Store does not render bullet characters as well as Play, and reviewers
prefer prose. Same substance, tighter.

```
Pirith Pothaa brings the Pirith chanting tradition to your phone, in a form
made for everyday listening.

Open the app and play. The chanting continues while you cook, travel, work or
fall asleep — audio keeps playing when you leave the app or lock your screen,
with full controls on the lock screen, in Control Centre, and through
Bluetooth headphones or CarPlay-connected stereos.

Listen to chants from short suttas to the full Maha Piritha. Download any of
them and play with no connection at all — on a flight, in a signal-free area,
or without spending mobile data twice. Build playlists and play them straight
through or shuffled, repeat a single chant, or loop a whole list. A sleep
timer stops the chanting on its own so you can fall asleep to it.

Every screen shows Sinhala and English together. Chant names appear in Sinhala
with the English name beneath, so the app suits Sinhala speakers and anyone

The player is deliberately calm: large artwork, a clear progress bar, and
controls where your thumb already is. One tap plays. Nothing gets in the way
of the chanting.

There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address
or phone number. Your playlists, favourites, listening history and downloads

May the blessings of the Pirith be with you.
```

## Data safety / App Privacy answers

Declare **collected**: anonymous app ID, usage/analytics data, crash logs,
advertising ID — all "not linked to identity", none used for tracking.

Do **not** declare playlists, favourites, history or downloads. They never
leave the device, and declaring them as collected would be inaccurate.
